tips.tasti.io

Tips, ready for payroll

live

A week of tips across four rooms. The missed clock-out and the owner behind the bar are decided by a person, not guessed by a formula. Split to the cent by written rules, signed off per room, and every member of staff gets their own working on their phone.

Replaces
A manager, a spreadsheet and two hours per room every pay period
Time
about 2 hours per room per pay period
The point
At 4 rooms, 2 hours each, 26 pay periods and $35 an hour, that is about $7,300 a year of manager time, before the disputes
